Frequently Asked Questions

Got A Question?

What kind of food do you serve?

Johnny Guitar serves classic roadhouse comfort food including burgers, dagwoods, boerie rolls, Sunday lunches and milkshakes.

What are your opening hours?

Johnny Guitar is open every day from 9:15 am to 8:00 pm. On Fridays, we are open until 9:00 pm.

Where are you located?

We are located at 57 Voortrekker Road, New Redruth, Alberton, 1449, Gauteng, South Africa.

How can I place an order?

You can call us on 011 907 0379, send us a WhatsApp message, or email drivein@johnnyguitar.co.za.

How can I contact Johnny Guitar?

Call 011 907 0379, WhatsApp 060 788 3766, or email drivein@johnnyguitar.co.za.
